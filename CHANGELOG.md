# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- The panel now uses Drawdy's own colours and font, with a more compact header so more logos fit
- Category chips scroll with a fade at the edge, and selected options are grey instead of lime
- Logos that would vanish on their tile, such as a black mark in the dark theme, sit on a contrasting plate

## 1.0.0

First public release

- Over 3,400 Simple Icons brand logos plus full-colour LinkedIn, Slack, Microsoft and Adobe logos, in ten categories for marketing work, with ranked search that knows short forms like `ig`
- Brand colours by default, or ink or any colour, four sizes and a name label
- Drag a logo onto the board or click to add it in view; repeated clicks line up in rows
